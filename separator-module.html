Vertical three-phase (gas/oil/water) separator. Diameter is governed by whichever is largest of: gas capacity (Souders-Brown Ks), or the minimum diameter needed to keep the combined liquid's superficial velocity below the water-in-oil settling velocity or the oil-in-water rising velocity (a conservative check — both droplet types are checked against the full combined liquid rate). Layer heights then follow from retention time at that diameter.

Gas filter-separator (coalescing filter elements + mist eliminator, typically horizontal). The filter elements — not gravity settling — do the fine-mist removal, so this uses a generic, publicly-cited higher Ks (~1.3 ft/s per GPSA Ch. 7 for filter-separator vessels) for a preliminary housing diameter, plus liquid-holding sizing. Filter-element face-area/count is inherently vendor-specific (Peco, Facet, Pall, Croft, etc.) — enter your vendor's rated capacity per element if you have a datasheet; otherwise leave blank and confirm with the vendor before finalizing.
