SEZTools — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

SEZTools ("the App") is a set of offline engineering calculation tools (Pump Hydraulic Calc. and Line Sizing) for process engineers.

Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics. All calculations run entirely on your device. The App does not require an internet connection to function and does not communicate with any server.

Data You Enter

Any values you enter (equipment data, fluid properties, line sizes, etc.) stay on your device only, for the duration of your session, and are used solely to generate the calculation results and optional PDF/Excel exports you create yourself. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Permissions

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other device data.

Third Parties

The App includes no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking libraries of any kind.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer via the app's GitHub repository.
